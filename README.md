## The entry

> 🌌

One emoji, nothing else. `main.py` writes it to `/output/entry.txt`.

## Why this emoji

🌌 is the Milky Way — the night sky over the Arctic on an aurora-chasing trip. Not a beach, not a postcard anyone could send from anywhere: one cold, clear night in Tromsø, green light rippling overhead, the single journey on the bucket list that no sunny resort can replace. It is a dream you have to travel for, in the dark, looking up — and a stranger sees the whole trip in it at a glance.
